# SeaWatch Product Context

## Product name

SeaWatch

## Core idea

A conversational, map-first marine intelligence experience where users can inspect any relevant area of sea and specialised intelligence collaborates to explain opportunities, conditions, restrictions, and hazards.

## Primary audiences

1. Fisherman
2. Coastal resident or local community
3. Marine or port authority

## Primary demo context

- Geography: Kochi and the surrounding Kerala coast and Arabian Sea
- Medium: Laptop presentation and recorded demo video
- Primary viewport: Approximately 1440 × 900
- Implementation: A frontend prototype in which data, collaboration, voice, and interactions may be deterministic and simulated

## Inspiration

The intuitive spatial discoverability of Snap Map, translated into a serious marine intelligence product without imitating Snapchat visually.

## Core philosophy

The user asks one question of the ocean rather than manually interrogating multiple fragmented marine datasets.

The ocean is the primary interface. Land provides geographic orientation, while ports, coastal locations, relevant vessels, marine conditions, restrictions, hazards, geofences, and advisories appear progressively when useful.

SeaWatch may support fisherman, coastal-resident, and marine-authority perspectives without exposing underlying technical architecture to the user.
