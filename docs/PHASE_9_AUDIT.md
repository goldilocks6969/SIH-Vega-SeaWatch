# Phase 9 Visual Audit

Audit target: the live 1440×900 SeaWatch prototype across rest, ocean selection, fishing analysis, fishing answer, cyclone inspection, disaster build/peak/final, affected-vessel review, and Malayalam voice.

## Initial critique

- The ocean was an even blue wash. It had texture, but not enough depth, shelf structure, or low-frequency tonal separation to feel like a living regional sea.
- The cyclone was the weakest visual object: bright nested strokes made it read as a drawn hurricane symbol and competed with every analysis state.
- Resting, analytical, and answered states shared nearly the same contrast hierarchy. Data was present at the peak but the composition did not clearly declare a peak.
- Answer and inspector surfaces were competent but generic. Their fully enclosed pale rectangles felt placed over the map rather than composed with it.
- The agent strip was coherent but too faint at rest and insufficiently authoritative during analysis. Activity copy was below comfortable demo-distance reading size.
- Scientific map labels were mostly well anchored, but disaster labels at 9.5px and several low-contrast metadata lines weakened legibility in a room-scale demonstration.
- Fishing fields were visually useful but relied on clean nested geometry. The blur and final-state reduction needed more organic separation.
- Composer suggestions looked like UI chips, adding avoidable product chrome to the most important empty ocean region.
- The Malayalam voice state was emotionally strong and human; it needed preservation rather than a different treatment.

## Final decisions

- Added deep-water masses, coastal shelf light, restrained bathymetric traces, and independent 48/56-second drift below the data plane.
- Rebuilt the cyclone art direction around asymmetric diffuse cloud lobes and blurred independent bands; coral remains limited to the eye.
- Changed answer and object-detail surfaces to single-edge editorial washes that dissolve into the ocean.
- Increased answer scale to 28px, disaster annotations to 10.25px, and working-agent microcopy to 10px.
- Added a quiet connective hairline to the agent strip, strengthened active-role contrast, and receded irrelevant roles during work.
- Replaced circular ripple geometry with incomplete elliptical water-wave fragments and an irregular persistent selection.
- Removed filled suggestion chips in favor of quiet text invitations with hairline feedback.
- Retained sparse open water, the warm subordinate land mass, existing information architecture, deterministic scenarios, and all interaction behavior.
